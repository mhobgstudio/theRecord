# README.md

This document is an attempt to map out Islamic knowledge in a structured way (NOT-COMPLETED) based only on Quran and Hadith. You may take your time to read it from the beginning by zooming out for a full overview, or use **Ctrl+F** (or **Cmd+F on Mac)** to search for specific topics you are interested in from the Table of Contents. Arabic appears alongside English to preserve scriptural references, with Quranic verses and Hadith cited throughout. In some places, reflective narrative passages (e.g., from the perspective of the Pen or Adam) are used to inspire contemplation. Whether read sequentially or explored by topic, the text is designed to guide reflection and understanding step by step.
